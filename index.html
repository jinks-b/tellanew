A journal you talk to

Journaling by typing asks you to compose. Journaling by talking just asks you to show up. Tell Anew records short video entries, transcribes them on your iPhone, and titles them from what you actually said.

  • Transcripts are free, on every entry, kept forever. Transcription runs on the device itself.
  • Your entries are yours whether or not you pay. Viewing, playing, exporting and deleting always work.
  • Time capsules. Seal an entry with a date and it comes back on the day you chose.

Check it yourself

  • Turn on airplane mode, then record, read the transcript and search. It all still works.
  • Export your whole journal from Settings, any time, for free. You get one zip file. Inside, each entry has its own folder with the original video or audio, a subtitle file (.srt), and a data file (.json) with the date, title, and full transcript. Time capsules stay sealed until the day they open, so they're left out until then.
