How it works

Some of the best parts of Tell Anew are easy to miss. Each of these is under a minute, with sound.

Don't know what to say Already have the moment Want it back For later After a few weeks

When you don't know what to say

Let it ask you

Not sure what to say? Tap today's question and it walks you through a few more as you talk. Afterwards, each question sits in your transcript above your answer.

Check in

Some days, one word is enough. Pick how today sat, add a line if you want, or talk about it for up to a minute.

When you already have the moment

Talk over a photo

Pick a photo and tell its story. It lands on the day the photo was taken, and however long you talk is how long it lasts.

Talk over a video

Filmed something but didn't say a word? Add it from your camera roll, record your voice over it, and slide some of the room's own sound back in under your voice.

When you want it back

Find the exact second

Search one word you remember. The video opens at the sentence where you said it, and tapping any line of the transcript jumps right there.

For later

Ask your future self something

Type or record a question and pick the day it comes back. Until then it stays sealed. On that day it is waiting over your camera, in your own words.

After a few weeks

Some things only show up once you've been talking for a while.

  • Old entries come back. Now and then the journal brings back one old entry and says why, like a year ago today.
  • A Monday recap. What you talked about the week before, in a line, and a way to watch it. No targets, no streaks.
  • A month, finished. When a month ends, it offers the month back to watch.
  • This day, every year. Hold any day in the calendar to see that date in every year you've recorded.
